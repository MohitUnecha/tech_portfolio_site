Growing Up Desi: Where Approval Is on Backorder

Posted: Nov 25, 2025 · Creative Writing

Episode: Surviving a Stereotypical Desi Household

In this podcast episode, I talk about what it is like growing up in a stereotypical Desi household where approval always feels just out of reach. From grades and careers to expectations at home, it feels like there is always another standard to meet.

I mix humor and honesty to explore pressure, culture, love, and the constant chase for validation. This episode is about laughing at the chaos, reflecting on childhood patterns, and imagining a healthier meaning of “proud of you.”
